# edit reel

**A Claude Code skill that turns any video into a finished Instagram reel, in one of three signature styles.**
Paste a link on your Reel Studio page (Google Drive, Dropbox, YouTube, a direct link) or drop files, pick the look,
how much motion graphics and how much sound you want, and Claude does the editing. You review each draft on a page
with Keep / Change / Drop buttons.

| Style | Vibe |
|---|---|
| 🤍 **it girl** | clean editorial: white serif captions, the key line big, butter-yellow chapter titles |
| ✨ **whimsical** | warm glow, chunky cream + butter-yellow words, twinkling asterisks, pill labels |
| 💎 **scrapbook** | a journal page that talks: a pinned title per idea in mixed fonts (handwritten + chunky lavender), sparkle bursts, shiny 3D gems, handwritten asides, quiet subtitles |

What it does for you:
- Picks the best take of every line across all your clips and cuts the dead air
- Send it a reel you love ("make it look like this") and it studies the look before editing
- Frames your face and adds gentle punch-ins between takes
- Word-by-word animated captions in your language, placed so they **never cover your face**
- An animated hook title for the first seconds, stickers and transitions
- 17 video effects: punch zoom, zoom-through cut, checklist, floating phone, phone frame, pull-back reveal, app split,
  note wall, headline wall, chart grow, orbit, new background, paper cutout, doodle outline, dolly zoom, cover title
  and glass title (text behind your head)
- Motion graphics placed automatically from what you say (or on the music's beat): light, medium or max
- Sound effects only where they add impact: the intro, bullet points, key transitions and punchlines (16 sounds,
  synthesised, royalty-free), off / subtle / normal / bold, or swap in your own; loudness levelled for Instagram
- The intro title appears word by word with your voice; every cut lands with a gentle punch-in
- Works with any video: vertical or landscape, with or without a face, with or without speech
- Checks every cut and the audio before it hands you the video
- A review page for every draft: play it, press Keep / Change / Drop on each take, graphic and effect, retype misheard
  captions, switch the look and pin notes to exact moments. Claude reads your choices and makes the next draft.

## Requirements
- A Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) or Linux. Windows is not supported yet.
- [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) (desktop app or terminal)
- Python 3.9+ and ffmpeg (Mac: `brew install python ffmpeg`)
- Internet once, for setup (Python packages, fonts and the speech-to-text model)

## Install
Copy the `edit-reel` folder into `~/.claude/skills/` so you have `~/.claude/skills/edit-reel/SKILL.md`.
Then open Claude Code and type:

```
/edit-reel
```

The first run sets everything up (about 2 minutes) and asks you a few questions (your default style, the language
you speak in your videos). After that, just say what you want:

> /edit-reel my clips are in this drive folder: https://drive.google.com/... use the scrapbook style

The first run also gives you your **Reel Studio** page: paste links there any time (or share it with your team),
then tell Claude `check my studio`.

## Tips for great results
- Film vertical (9:16), face roughly in the middle, some space above or below your head for the words.
- Say each line until you are happy with it; the skill keeps the last clean take.
- Share Google Drive folders as **Anyone with the link → Viewer**.
