# Effects library (17)

Effects go in `plan.json` → `"effects": [...]`, each `{"type": "...", "start": s, "end": e, ...options}` in reel
seconds. They work with every style; the style's captions are drawn on top (some effects hide or move them).
All graphics are generated by code; images you pass (`image`, `images`) are the user's own files, relative to the
project folder (put them in `<project>/photos/`).

Effects marked **cutout** need the speaker cut out of the background: after editing plan.json, run
`PY SK/scripts/cutout.py <project>` (it only processes the frames inside those effects, ~0.3-1 s per frame on a
laptop CPU; faster on Apple Silicon). Without it they fall back to drawing in front, away from the face.

## When to use what (one effect per idea, 2-6 per reel, never two at once)
| Moment in the script | Effect |
|---|---|
| The key word / punchline | `punch-zoom` (with `text`) |
| Jump between two sections | `zoom-through` |
| "Here's my process / steps" | `checklist` |
| Showing an app, a message, a result | `phone-float`, `app-split` |
| "This is what my feed / reel looks like" | `phone-frame` |
| The ending / reveal of the work | `pull-back` |
| Overwhelm, noise, many thoughts | `headline-wall`, `note-wall` |
| Growth, results, numbers | `chart-grow` |
| Showing off your content | `orbit` |
| Mood shift, "imagine..." | `new-background`, `dolly-zoom` |
| Playful, crafty moment | `paper-cutout`, `doodle-outline` |
| Big title / the name of your method | `cover-title`, `glass-title` |

## Reference
1. **punch-zoom**: fast punch-in on the face for a beat. `zoom` (1.3), `text` ("line one|line two": cream then
   butter yellow, set low). Hides captions while text shows.
2. **zoom-through**: transition through a cut: rush in with a zoom blur, land out of the blur in the next shot.
   `at` (cut time; default nearest cut), `half` (0.22 s each side).
3. **phone-frame**: the video shrinks into a phone-screen card with reel icons and `@handle` over a dark blurred
   copy. `handle` (default from config), `scale` (0.72).
4. **pull-back**: reveal the video playing inside an editor window on cream paper, a task list ticking off and a
   counter counting up. `title`, `items[]`, `count`, `label`. Hides captions. Great as the ending.
5. **app-split**: the video slides down; an app panel fills the top: clips drop into a drop zone and a prompt types
   itself. `app`, `prompt`, `images[]` (default: stills from the reel), `height` (0.36). Hides captions.
6. **dolly-zoom** (cutout): vertigo: the room zooms while you stay the same size. `amount` (0.35), `direction`
   (in|out).
7. **checklist**: frosted dark card at the top; items tick off with a yellow check. `title`, `items[]`, `at[]`
   (tick times; default spread out). Captions move to the bottom.
8. **phone-float**: a phone floats in beside you with a screenshot (`image`) or a chat that writes itself
   (`messages[]`, alternating sides), `title`. Captions move to the top.
9. **note-wall** (cutout): notes pop onto the wall behind you one by one. `notes[]` or `images[]`, `every` (0.35 s).
10. **headline-wall** (cutout): rapid-fire newspaper-style headlines behind you. `headlines[]`, `every` (0.22 s).
11. **chart-grow** (cutout): a glowing line chart draws itself behind you and lands on a label pill. `label`,
    `labels[]` (x axis, default M-S), `points[]` (0-1).
12. **orbit** (cutout): your photos/clips circle around you, passing behind and in front. `images[]` (default:
    stills from the reel), `count` (6), `speed`.
13. **new-background** (cutout): swap the room behind you with a wipe. `image` or `preset`: studio | paper | blur | dark.
14. **paper-cutout** (cutout): you become a paper cut-out with a thick white border on a collage page (scribbles,
    highlighter, tape, red marks) with cut-out letters. `text` (e.g. "100%"), `seed`.
15. **doodle-outline** (cutout): a hand-drawn line traces around you and "boils" like animation, plus doodles by
    your head. `doodles[]` from heart, sparkle, star, swirl, arrow, clapper; `color`, `accent`.
16. **cover-title** (cutout): a magazine-cover word set huge behind your head, letters rising. `text`, `kicker`,
    `color`. Hides captions.
17. **glass-title** (cutout): a big 3D chrome-glass word stands behind you, letters sliding up. `text`, `tint`
    ([r, g, b] 0-1). Hides captions.

## Sound
Every effect plays its own sounds automatically (`scripts/effects/sound.py`): e.g. punch-zoom = riser + impact,
zoom-through = whoosh, checklist = pop + a ding per tick, phone-float = swish + a notification per message,
walls = a pop per card, cover-title = impact + shutter, glass-title = chime + impact. plan.json `"sfx"` scales all of
them: off | subtle | normal | bold.

## Automatic placement
`autofx.py --amount light|medium|max` places effects and stickers from the transcript (and music beats). Treat its
output as a first pass: rewrite texts, remove what does not fit, add what the user asked for.

## Checking
Snap the middle of every effect (`render.py --at`) and look: no text or card on the face, nothing outside the safe
area, the cutout edge clean (hair/hijab). If a cutout edge looks rough, shorten the effect or pick one that does not
need it.
